A little bit about me

Hi, I'm Blebbyboi, and I'm an indie developer who likes experimenting with random stuff as well as trying out other people's creations!

What I do, and how I do it

So, as I said above I like coding. I can code decent GDScript (the language for the game engine Godot), but I still have a lot to learn. I also can code a teeeensy bit of Python, even though it has similar syntax to GDScript. However, I have yet to learn C, C++, and Assembly. I am learning binary though! (even if that might be in... sub-conventional ways.)

I can also do a little bit of art, although not very good. I do have experience with MIDI though, as I do like messing around with video game soundfonts. For this I use LMMS.

What I like to do

As I said before, I like experimenting with random stuff, such as creating baby-AIs that can learn, and other random Discord bots and stuff.

I also like messing around with code, for example, the Super Mario 64 source code, to exploit memory leaks to get optimal speedrun times.

About that, one of my favourite things to do is play video games! Specifically Super Mario 64, which I like to speedrun, Minecraft, where I like to build binary redstone stuff.

I also like playing other indie games, such as Undertale, Deltarune, Slime Rancher, Hollow Knight and Terraria.
